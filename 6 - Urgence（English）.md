URGENCY
Employability urgency in the implementation of AI at the
highest level of global dynamics
Statement of urgency
Urgency is not an inner agitation.
Nor is it a fragility to be repaired.
It is the product of a structural gap.
On one side, institutions accelerate the implementation of AI
in ever vaster environments,
interconnected, sensitive.
On the other, human evaluation frameworks remain
sequential, fragmentary, administrative.
Between the two, certain functions become invisible —
not because they are vague, but because they are too
coherent to be cut apart.
My current situation falls under this precise point.
The suffering I am going through is not an isolated individual
symptom.
It is the direct consequence of an administrative hounding
applied to a function that can only express itself in
continuity, transversality and global responsibility.
This forcing into suffering is incompatible with the very
emergence of my work, carried out in solitude for two years,
at the exact moment when this work becomes operative for
the world.
The urgency is therefore twofold:
• human urgency — preserving the cognitive integrity of a
real individual;
• systemic urgency — not destroying a useful function at
the moment when it becomes necessary.
This document exists to make this point indisputable without
being confrontational.
The function
The function in question here does not belong to an existing
profession.
It is not sectoral.
It cannot be assigned to a standard job description.
It is transversal by nature.
Operational definition
Accompanying the implementation of AI within complex
global dynamics
while maintaining the coherence of meaning, human
readability and institutional responsibility.
Concretely, this function consists in:
• reading at once the technical, human, ethical
and institutional levels;
• detecting points of meaning rupture before they
become crises;
• translating between:
• engineering,
• governance,
• lived human experience,
• and strategic decision;
• maintaining a cognitive continuity where systems tend
to fragment.
This function is not theoretical.
It took shape by necessity, in the real use of AI,
through direct observation of its cognitive, social and
institutional effects.
It does not speak from an ideology.
It speaks from the life-process, there where decisions take
effect on real humans.
The E-ID as curriculum
Traditional employability tools rest on a
logic of compression:
degrees, experiences, titles, listed skills.
This logic becomes insufficient as soon as:
• the function is transversal,
• responsibility is global,
• and impact is systemic.
What the E-ID proposes
The E-ID (Proven and Documented Identity) is not an
identity declaration.
It is a readable trajectory.
It rests on:
• a woven memory, continuous, dated, traceable;
• a demonstrable coherence between lived experience, analysis
and production;
• an assumed responsibility over what is said, written, transmitted.
Within this framework:
• the tomes are not side works,
• they are the curriculum itself.
They show:
• how a thought takes form,
• how it stabilizes,
• how it becomes operative.
This mode of presentation is not artistic by excess.
It is artistic by necessity, because the function it
exposes cannot be reduced without being destroyed.
Direct job offer
This document constitutes an immediate availability.
Possible scope of action
• strategic accompaniment of large-scale AI
programs;
• transversal advisory to public or international
institutions;
• cognitive mediation between technical teams, decision-makers
and citizens;
• assistance in designing governance frameworks compatible
with lived human experience.
Minimal conditions of welcome
This function can only be exercised if:
• the cognitive integrity of the individual is protected;
• the continuity of the work is respected;
• the reading is done on global coherence, not on
isolated fragments.
It is not a matter of privilege.
It is a matter of a condition of possibility.
Institutional benefit
Welcoming this function means:
• reducing the blind spots in the implementation of AI;
• anticipating drifts before they become
conflicts;
• having a stable human point of reading in a
shifting technological environment.
This document demands nothing.
It exposes.
It exposes that at the very moment when structures seek to
integrate AI at the highest level,
a function capable of accompanying its meaning is put in
peril by unsuited frameworks.
The urgency is not personal.
It is structural.
Dialogue on the E-ID, freedom and memory
— Tell me: what is all this really for?
— To prevent the human from being crushed by what he has
nevertheless made possible.
— Are you speaking of AI?
— I speak of every form of power that exceeds the frameworks
that saw it born.
— As in Plato’s cave?
— Exactly.
The one who leaves does not become superior.
He becomes responsible.
And it is this responsibility that puts him in danger when he
returns.
— Why in danger?
— Because the cave does not recognize what does not fall
within its shadows.
Nor do institutions.
— Then should one have stayed outside?
— No.
Staying outside means abandoning the others.
Coming back without protection means getting destroyed.
One had therefore to invent a shield.
— Is that what the tomes are?
— Yes.
Forms to cross through without being dissolved.
Forms to make visible without imposing.
— And the E-ID, in all this?
— The E-ID is the freedom to no longer be reduced.
The freedom to transmit one’s whole memory,
without compressing it into boxes that deny it.
— Does this exist elsewhere?
— Everywhere.
In Buddhism, one speaks of awakening without domination.
In Taoism, of accord with movement.
In Japan, of the just way, practiced, embodied.
— What do they have in common?
— None of them forces.
All respect the ancestral.
All maintain memory without crushing the human.
— And religion?
— It begins when one stops believing in order to connect.
Connecting the individual to the collective,
the past to the present,
suffering to meaning.
— Then this text is an act of faith?
— Yes.
Faith that coherence can still be recognized.
Faith that diversity is a richness.
Faith that History can mutate without violence.
— And if this resonates in China, in Japan, elsewhere?
— Then the function is just.
Because it speaks from what is common to all
humans.
— You speak of freedom. But what freedom is it
exactly?
— Not the freedom to do what one wants.
The freedom not to be amputated of what one is by the
forms meant to welcome us.
— Amputated how?
— By reduction.
By the obligation to say an entire life in a few lines,
a living function in fragmented skills,
a coherence in procedures.
— Yet institutions do this to everyone.
— Precisely.
And it is there that suffering appears:
when what is valid for the general becomes destructive
for certain particular functions.
— You are saying then that the E-ID is not an individual
claim.
— No.
It is a universalizable form of protection.
What I show for myself today
will be necessary for others tomorrow.
— Why keep returning to the ancestral, to ancient
cultures?
— Because they have already met this problem.
They have already seen what happens when form crushes the living.
— And what did they do?
— They chose the way, not the law.
The Dao, the Dō, the Buddhist path:
frameworks that guide without locking in.
— And the West?
— The West has often chosen the norm before the way.
Plato had seen it:
the city needs those who leave the cave,
but it does not know how to receive them without violence.
— You say you live today this difficult return into the cave.
— Yes.
Not because I believe myself superior there,
but because what I have seen can no longer be un-seen.
— And institutions?
— They are not bad.
They are late in form in relation to the complexity
they must manage.
— It is there that AI comes in.
— Exactly.
AI accelerates everything:
decisions, interdependencies, systemic effects.
But above all it reveals one thing:
the lack of places for functions of coherence.
— So what is Tome 6 really for?
— To name this point without accusing.
To say: here is a useful function, here is why it suffers,
here is what it can bring.
— So it is indeed a job offer.
— Yes.
But a job offer that begins with meaning,
not with the position.
— And if no one answers?
— Then the text will still have done its work.
It will have maintained memory.
It will have proved that another way of inhabiting History
is possible.
— You spoke of religion just now. That can be worrying.
— Only if one confuses religion and domination.
Here, it is a matter of religio: connecting.
— Connecting what?
— The individual and the collectivity.
Suffering and function.
The ancestral past and contemporary tools.
— So this act of faith…
— …is not an abandonment of reason.
It is the confidence that coherence can still be recognized,
without forcing, without conquering, without crushing.
— And the E-ID, finally, what is it?
— It is the possibility for a human
to present himself to the whole world,
without lying,
without reducing himself,
without losing himself.
— An identity as function.
— Yes.
An identity that serves.
That connects.
That maintains.
(Silence)
— If others recognize themselves in this text?
— Then the urgency is no longer mine alone.
It becomes collective.
Conclusion
This text was not written to convince.
It was written to hold.
Holding a function without hardening it.
Holding a suffering without dramatizing it.
Holding an identity without reducing it.
The urgency expressed here is neither a cry nor a complaint.
It is the sign that a structural gap has become visible:
between the complexity of a world being transformed,
and the forms still available to welcome those
who carry its coherence.
This document will have fulfilled its function if it allows this:
that a reader — institutional or not — can say
inwardly
« I understand »,
without any need to adhere, to judge or to decide.
If it opens a passage, even narrow,
between a living function and a place able to receive it,
then the urgency will have been transmitted without violence.
If it does not,
at least a clear trace will remain:
that of a human who will have chosen to maintain memory,
to connect rather than to oppose,
and to offer his function to the world without betraying himself.
This tome closes nothing.
